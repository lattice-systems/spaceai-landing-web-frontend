# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two distinct logged-in audiences, no public/anonymous design surface in scope here:
- **Admin team (Lattice Systems staff)** — manages the full commercial backend of the SpaceIA
  ecosystem: users, catalog, materia prima, recetas, proveedores, compras, cotizaciones,
  reseñas, mensajes. Daily, task-driven use; density and speed matter more than delight.
- **Client accounts (university institutions)** — self-service portal to track their own
  cotizaciones, open support tickets, download documentation, leave reviews. Lower frequency,
  needs to feel trustworthy and clear, not necessarily dense.

## Product Purpose

SpaceIA (by Lattice Systems) is the commercial/admin platform for an intelligent campus
ecosystem (QR identity, access control, kiosks, robotics). This app is the operational tool
that runs the business side: selling, quoting, fulfilling, and supporting that ecosystem for
university clients. Success = admins can act fast on what needs attention (pending
cotizaciones, low stock, unread messages) and clients can self-serve without contacting support
for routine things.

## Brand Personality

"Tech confiable" — reliable, modern, precise. Not playful/startup-loud, not
institutional/stuffy-university. Feels like infrastructure you can trust to run a business on.

References given by the user:
- **Vercel dashboards** — clean, high-contrast, restrained color with sharp precision; mono
  for data/numbers; dark-friendly neutral surfaces over pure white.
- **Fly.io** — same family but "fresco" (fresher, a bit more alive) — willing to use a
  saturated accent with intent, not just monochrome.
- **Odoo** — referenced for logic/workflow density, not visual style: tight information
  density, functional groupings, no wasted chrome, everything reachable in few clicks.

## Anti-references

- The app's own current state: flat white background everywhere, no tonal variation, reads as
  generic/washed-out SaaS template.
- Generic AI-SaaS defaults: purple/blue gradient glows, gradient text, glassmorphism as
  decoration, warm beige/cream neutrals with no reason.
- Anything that reads as playful/consumer (this is B2B operational software, not a marketing
  site).

## Design Principles

1. **Neutral base, one confident accent** — tonal, slightly saturated neutrals (not flat
   white/gray) carry the UI; a single accent color does the pointing (primary actions, active
   state, key data), used consistently, not scattered.
2. **Data-forward, chrome-minimal** — favor tables, numbers, and real content density
   (Odoo-like) over decorative cards and padding; only add visual weight where it communicates
   hierarchy or state.
3. **Every screen already has real data and real actions** — this is a functionally complete
   app (9 admin modules + client portal, all wired to a real backend); a visual pass must not
   regress any working filter, dialog, or table interaction already built this session.
4. **Fresh but restrained** — willing to commit to color (Fly.io-like) rather than hide behind
   neutrals, but one accent hue, used with intent, not a rainbow of status colors per screen.
5. **Dashboards tell a story fast** — the admin/client dashboards are the one place where a
   more distinctive, attention-grabbing visualization is warranted; list/table screens stay
   calm and functional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA across both light and dark states: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text/UI
components ≥3:1. No stated special accommodation needs beyond that baseline.
